WHY ARE ROSES ARE SO POPULAR ?

Introduction:

Roses have captivated the hearts and senses of people around the world from centuries, becoming
the most popular flower in history. Their enduring popularity can be attributed to several factors.

Historical Significance:

Roses have been on-trend since as far back as 2000 BC, with evidence of their use in ancient
civilizations like Iraq and China. They were so valued that they were even used as legal
currency in trade1.

Symbolism:

Different colors of roses carry different meanings, but red roses, in particular, have become a
universal symbol of love and passion. This association is deeply rooted inmyths and legends,
such as the Greek myth of Aphrodite and Adonis, where red roses sprang from Aphrodite’s
tears and Adonis’s blood1.

Cultural Impact:

Roses have been used in medicine, beauty products, perfume, and decor for thousands of years.
Their influence extends across various cultures and practices, making them a staple in celebrations
and expressions of affection1.

Aesthetic Appeal:

The beauty of roses, with their vibrant colors and intricate petal structures, along with their
delightful fragrance, makes them a favorite choice for gardens, floral arrangements, and as a means of
expressing emotions.

Variety and Versatility:

With a wide range of colors and types, roses can suit any occasion, mood, or preference. They
are also relatively easy to care for, which adds to their appeal for both amateur and professional gardeners.

Presence in Public Spaces:

Public rose gardens, like those in Hartford, Connecticut, and Stanley Park in Vancouver, BC,
showcase the beauty of roses and contribute to their popularity by providing a space for people to enjoy these
flowers in a communal setting.
